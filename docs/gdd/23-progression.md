# 23 · Progression: Knowledge and Devices

## Two kinds of unlocks (decision A05)

- **Knowledge** is recovered (data caches, research sites, Research Puzzles, story) and unlocks recipes and features. It fits the lore: the robot is missing applied knowledge.
- **Devices** are built or found, and installed on a vehicle or at the base.

Some unlocks start a **Research Puzzle** (quiz or puzzle; unlimited attempts, no penalty). Multi-stage Knowledge has a puzzle per stage. Puzzle content: OQ-21.

**Reverse Engineering** can unlock technologies without their key item (see 21-economy).

How **Weight Classes** unlock is not decided yet (OQ-02).

The **free camera** is available from the start (decision C26).

## Knowledge

### Internal Ballistics
- Unlocks propellant improvements for Ballistic weapons: changes reload time, muzzle velocity, dispersion and firing Noise.

### External Ballistics
- Unlocks ammo variants beyond the default AP and HE: APCR, HEAT, APHE, HESH and other charge types.

### Control Theory
- Unlocks automated control technology.
- **Unlocks Overclocked mode for every weapon that allows it** (decision C08). Nothing can overclock before this.
- Weapons can take Target Lock and fire on player orders without crew (crew still gives bonuses).
- With the Satellite Uplink: Logistics Nodes and transports.

### Scrap Sorting Manual
- Main-story reward (decision C17).
- Unlocks Salvaging and collecting resources from scrap heaps.

### Radio Communications
- Brain-in-a-Vat vehicles accept the player's orders (Squads; see 17-controls).
- Remote operation of Logistics Node weapons; temporary changes to transport routes and destinations.
- **Radio chatter:** the player overhears radio traffic that hints at random events happening nearby (raids, caravans, wrecks, faction moves). Acting on these hints gives an advantage.

### Additive Manufacturing (decision C10)
- Unlocks the recipe for the **Material Printer** (Device).
- Unlocks independent manufacturing of composite materials.

### Computer Vision
- With Control Theory: autonomous ground suicide swarms that pick their own targets.
- With Control Theory and Aeronautics: autonomous drone swarms.

### Electromagnetics (decision G17)
- Coilgun / railgun family: electric Ballistic weapons with very high velocity and low Noise.
- Electric drive upgrades: better Diesel-Electric efficiency; in-wheel motors.
- Electronic Warfare: jammers and radar.
- EM shielding material: reduces EW effects on your vehicle.

### Wireless Power Transfer
- Electric Power Plants and Relays can feed specific parts without conduits; power still falls off with distance.

### Aeronautics
- Basic fixed-wing aircraft (including swarm drones) and basic multirotor aircraft — as transports and swarms only (decision C23).

### Organic Chemical Engineering
- Converts organic waste into fuel and other raw materials.
- Unlocks lightweight materials for transports.
- Converts functional crops into fuel and other materials.
- Unlocks certain synthesized items (high-value trade goods or side-quest requirements).
- Several stages, each with its own Research Puzzle.

### Advanced Metallurgy
- Unlocks better metals for weapons, armor and more, in stages.

## Devices

### Material Printer (decision C10)
- Built after Additive Manufacturing.
- Adjusts armor **infill**: less material and lighter blocks with equal or slightly worse stats.
- Changes the surface texture of blocks it prints, without changing other stats.

### Element Analyzer
- Shows which materials, and how much, can be salvaged from a wreck.

### Holo-Terminal
- Unlocks sharing blueprints (export/import; see 40-tech).
- With the Brain-Computer Interface: live view of vehicle status (loading state, part damage).

### Ballistic Computer
- Improves Target Lock accuracy of all friendly weapons.
- Shows where enemy weapons will hit, in real time.

### Night Vision Device
- Unlocks night vision and greatly improves the vehicle's view range at night.

### Brain-in-a-Vat
- Enables AI-controlled allied vehicles; each needs one Brain-in-a-Vat in its Command Module.
- Before Radio Communications they act on their own; after it they take Squad orders.
- The number of allied AI vehicles is tightly limited (OQ-15).

### Brain-Computer Interface
- Links with the Holo-Terminal for live vehicle status.
- Other effects to be redefined (OQ-04).

### Satellite Uplink
- Unlocks the minimap: unexplored areas show faint outlines and icons.
- Live regional weather on the minimap.
- With Control Theory: Logistics Nodes and transports.
