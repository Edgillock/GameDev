# 22 · Logistics

## Unlocking

- **Control Theory + Satellite Uplink:** the player can build basic Logistics Nodes (Depots) and ground transport vehicles.
- **+ Aeronautics:** basic transport aircraft and their variants, with Airfields.

## Transports

- Transport vehicles and aircraft are independent units. They only follow the routes the player sets between nodes (basic automatic pathfinding). They can be damaged by the player and by enemies.
- Some transports are modular: several modules, each with several variants. Variants change stats (energy use per distance, speed…) and the materials needed to build them. The player chooses every module's variant before manufacturing.
- Certain NPCs sell blueprints for other transport models. These usually aren't modular but outperform the self-built ones.

## Logistics Nodes

- Ground transports use **Depots**; aircraft use **Airfields**.
- A node can manufacture and repair transports, **repair** player vehicles (material cost scales with damage) and resupply player and allied AI vehicles (fuel, ammo…). It does not build new player vehicles (that's the Home Base).
- Each node can be fitted with several types and amounts of storage units to raise capacity.
- Nodes can have observation posts and defenses against **Raids**. Defenses use weapons from the vehicle weapon families.
- After **Radio Communications**, the player can remotely operate a node's weapons and temporarily change transport routes and destinations.
- Risk zones: building in riskier zones raises the chance of Raids and other random events.

## Aircraft scope (decision C23)

- Aircraft exist only as **transport aircraft** and as **drone swarms** launched from a vehicle module (see 23-progression: Computer Vision).
- No player-designed aircraft in v1.
