# 21 · Economy

## Currency (decision G05)

- **Scrip** is the basic currency.
- Traders also **barter**: they swap materials directly.
- Some synthesized items (Organic Chemical Engineering) serve as high-value trade goods or side-quest requirements.

## Materials and items (decision G06 — all sets)

| Set | Contents |
|---|---|
| Material tiers | Scrap → Steel → Alloy → Composite → Exotic |
| Fuels | Solid fuel (Steam Engines), Diesel / Biofuel (Combustion, Diesel-Electric), reactor fuel for Fission and Fusion Reactors (details OQ-18), Exotic Charge |
| Consumables | Ammo by type, coolant, repair kits, crew supplies |
| Crops | Functional crops from the Oasis → biofuel, chemicals, rations |

Materials and Scrip respawn in the world at intervals (in-game time).

## Farms and Extractors

Timed production of basic resources: crops from farms, fuel and ore from extractors.

## Home Base (decisions C06, C07)

- The **only place new player vehicles are manufactured**. Logistics Nodes repair, resupply and build transport vehicles, but not new player vehicles.
- The base is divided into fixed **sectors** (e.g. Foundry, Assembly, Power, Chemistry; final list OQ-20). The player spends resources to upgrade the sectors they need. Upgrades raise build speed and unlock part types.
- No factory-line layout puzzle.

## Reverse Engineering (decision A10)

- Spend materials on a **trial build** for a small chance to unlock a technology or item **without** its key item. Success unlocks it permanently.
- The chance for related technologies rises when you:
  - obtain related key items, meet certain NPCs, or fight certain battles;
  - engage, or get Detection on, an enemy or NPC vehicle carrying that weapon (each time adds a little);
  - collect certain items at POIs or tech caches.
- The bonus has a cap.

## Salvaging

- Unlocked by the **Scrap Sorting Manual**, given as a main-story reward (decision C17).
- The **Salvage Rig** is mounted on the front of a vehicle. It deals low, continuous kinetic damage to wrecks and collects materials; its size sets collection rate and damage per second.
- Also unlocks collecting resources from scrap heaps.
- The **Element Analyzer** shows which materials, and how much, a wreck will yield.

## Delivery Contracts

Carry supplies, valuables or goods to a location. Requires installing a specific mandatory cargo module. Rewards: special items or unlocked content.

## Crew Drills

Low-pressure minigames. Each drill gives XP to one crew role. Limited plays per period; refreshed on in-game time. Drill list: OQ-21.

## NPC trade

Neutral NPCs trade resources. Some NPCs may, by chance, offer content or resources that are unavailable in the story or ahead of the current story progress.
