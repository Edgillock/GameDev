# 25 · Story and campaign flow

## Protagonist

An embodied AI (robot), activated at factory settings just as the war began. Its data link was cut, so it has theoretical knowledge but almost no applied knowledge. It becomes a vehicle architect and commander. Name and story beats: OQ-05.

## Crew lore

Mixed human and robot crews (see 16-crew, OQ-06).

## Campaign flow (decision G09)

- **Region 1 is the tutorial arc.** First T1 build, power and armor basics, first fight, first Research Puzzle. Ends with the road to Region 2.
- **Region 2 is the hub.** Logistics Nodes and Control Theory; first fights with a Squad; the road splits to Regions 3 and 4.
- **Branch choice.** The player picks Region 3 or 4 first. Each gates different Knowledge; both are needed for the largest Weight Classes and the finale.
- **Length target.** Main story 12–15 hours; full completion around 40 hours.
- How Weight Classes unlock along this path: OQ-02.

## Endings (decision C18)

One finale. The epilogue changes with the side quests the player completed.

## Post-game

After the finale, the map spawns advanced challenges and NPCs that unlock advanced technology. Details: OQ-22. (Expeditions are not planned.)

## Ambient story

- Story logs in ruined towns.
- Faction radio chatter; after Radio Communications it also hints at random events the player can exploit (see 23-progression).
